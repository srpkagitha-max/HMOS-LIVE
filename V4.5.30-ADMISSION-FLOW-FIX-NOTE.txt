HMOS V4.5.30 — Admission Flow Correction

This fixes the V4.5.29 issue where the old admission markup was still rendering.

Online Admission:
1. Bed Selection is now physically rendered before Fee Details.
2. Selecting a bed stores the complete room record, including sharing capacity/type.
3. Selected room immediately applies its sharing-wise fee.
4. Total Fees is locked/read-only.
5. Amount Paying Now remains editable.
6. Balance recalculates from the selected sharing fee.
7. Summary shows Floor / Room / Sharing / Bed / occupancy / fee.
8. Admission is blocked until a bed is selected and its sharing fee is configured.

Manual Admission:
- Same room-first flow.
- Total fee auto-fills and is locked.
- Amount Received is editable.
- Balance is shown and auto-updates.

Version/cache bumped to 4.5.30.
