HMOS V4.5.29 — Room-First Admission Fee Flow

Changes:
1. Bed Selection is intended to appear before Fee Details.
2. Selecting a room/bed automatically applies that room's sharing-wise fee.
3. Total Fees is read-only/locked so it cannot be accidentally changed.
4. Amount Paying Now stays editable.
5. Balance updates automatically.
6. Bed summary shows Room / Sharing Type / Bed / Occupancy / Fee.
7. Admission cannot continue without a selected bed.
8. Admission cannot continue if the selected room's sharing fee is not configured.
9. Existing sharing-wise fee settings from V4.5.26+ remain the single fee source.

Recommended QA:
- Set Single / 2 / 3 / 4 Sharing fees.
- Open admission form.
- Select a 2 Sharing bed and verify total fee auto-fills and is locked.
- Change Amount Paying Now and verify Balance.
- Try submitting without a bed and confirm it is blocked.
