HMOS V4.5.36 — 5-Step Admission Form

Online Admission is now presented as a guided 5-step flow:

Step 1 — Basic Details
Student, course, phone, parent/guardian, joining date and address.

Step 2 — Bed Selection
One Select Floor, Room & Bed button plus one compact summary box.

Step 3 — Fees
Auto-filled Total Fees, Amount Paying Now and auto Balance.

Step 4 — Payment
Existing UPI/QR/Transaction ID payment flow, grouped under one clear step.

Step 5 — Check Admission Approval
Kept below the Submit for Admin Approval button.
Only:
- Check Admission Approval
- Phone search box
- Check Status button

No Firestore rules change required.
